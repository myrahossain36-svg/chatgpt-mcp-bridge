# Things to try

Copy any of these into Claude once the bridge is installed.

## Cross-checking

- "Answer this, then get ChatGPT's second opinion and tell me where you disagree: is it better to pay off a 4% mortgage early or invest?"
- "Draft a reply to this email, then have ChatGPT critique the tone before you show me the final version."
- Use the **cross_check** prompt from the prompt menu with any question.

## Coding

- "Run chatgpt_code_review on this function, focusing on security and tests."
- "Write a regex for UK postcodes, then ask ChatGPT to find inputs that break it."
- Use the **review_my_code** prompt to get both models' reviews merged.

## Thinking and writing

- "Start a conversation with ChatGPT called `novel` and brainstorm plot twists with it for five rounds, then summarise the best three."
- "Ask ChatGPT to debate against my plan to move our team to a four-day week."
- Use the **council** prompt for any decision where you want two independent approaches.

## Data

- "Use chatgpt_structured to turn this list of invoices into JSON with supplier, date, amount and VAT."
- "Have ChatGPT generate 20 realistic test customer records as JSON."

## Models and images

- "Compare gpt-5.6-sol and gpt-6.1-terra on this logic puzzle and tell me which reasoned better."
- "Which OpenAI models can I use? Which is the cheapest chat model?"
- "Generate a landscape image of a cosy bookshop on a rainy evening, then describe it."
